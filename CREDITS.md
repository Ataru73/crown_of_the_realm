# Credits

## Art

All images in `public/assets/` were generated with Google Gemini (gemini.google.com)
from the prompts in `tools/assets/prompts.yaml`, then cropped, keyed and
palette-reduced by `tools/assets/process.py`.

## Music

The tracks in `public/assets/music/` were generated with Lyria in the Gemini app
(gemini.google.com) from the prompts in `tools/assets/music-prompts.yaml`, then
looped, loudness-normalised and encoded by `tools/assets/music.py`.

## Sound effects

All sounds in `public/assets/sfx/` are synthesised from noise and sine partials by
`tools/assets/sfx.py`; no recordings or third-party samples are used.
